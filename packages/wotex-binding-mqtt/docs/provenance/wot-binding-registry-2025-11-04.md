# WoT Binding Registry status

## Observation

- Observed: 2026-09-02
- Source: [Web of Things Binding Registry](https://www.w3.org/TR/wot-binding-registry/)
- Published status shown: W3C Draft Registry, 04 November 2025

The Registry states that it is in a pilot phase, is not finalized and stable,
and remains work in progress. Publication as a Draft Registry does not imply
W3C endorsement.

The [2026 Web of Things Working Group charter](https://www.w3.org/2026/07/wot-wg-2026.html)
lists the MQTT Binding as an Editor's Draft with expected completion in Q4 2026;
its timeline plans publication as a Note in Q4 2026. At this observation date,
that is a plan rather than a completed publication.

This library therefore records provenance without presenting itself as a
registered binding, a certification tool, or evidence of conformance.
