# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Contributors must use welcoming and inclusive language, respect differing
viewpoints, accept constructive criticism, focus on what is best for the
community, and show empathy toward other community members.

Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private harassment,
publishing another person's private information without permission, and other
conduct reasonably considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to hello@wotex.io. Maintainers will
review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy, and may remove, edit, or
reject contributions or participation that violate this code.

This code is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
