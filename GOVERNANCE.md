# Governance

WoTEx is maintained in public under the `wotex-project` organization. All
packages in this repository share this governance; each package keeps its own
version, changelog and release decision.

Decisions are evidence-led and recorded in package specifications
(`docs/packages/<name>/specs/`), package decision records
(`docs/packages/<name>/decisions/`) or family decision records
(`docs/decisions/`).

The maintainers may accept, revise or reject a change based on specification
compatibility, conformance evidence, security, maintenance burden, standards
provenance and the consumer-neutral, process-free package boundary. Stable
compatibility promises require a released version of the package concerned
and cannot be inferred from a development branch or from another package's
release.

Contact: `hello@wotex.io`.
