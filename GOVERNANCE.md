# Governance

Wotex is maintained in public under the `wotex-project` organization. Decisions
are evidence-led and recorded in repository specifications or decision records.

The maintainers may accept, revise, or reject a change based on specification
compatibility, conformance evidence, security, maintenance burden, and the
consumer-neutral boundary. Stable compatibility promises require a released
version and cannot be inferred from a development branch.

Contact: `hello@wotex.io`.
